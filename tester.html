Test any deployed Gradio app

Browser-only · uses @gradio/client

How this works. Paste the URL of any deployed Gradio Space (yours, or one from the directory). The page connects via the official @gradio/client, lists the available API endpoints, and lets you fire a real request. Useful for sanity-checking a deploy before you wire it into your app.
